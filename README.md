## Changes made by the pack
### Seasons and food
- Month lenght is set to 180 days, or 2.5 irl days. an in-game year therefore takes 30 irl days
- Light sources last 22 times longer
- Food decay is set to 0.05 (same as the old server)

### Plants and animals
- Wild crops and bushes are 8 times rarer
- Wild fruit trees are 3 times rarer
- Crops take 2 times longer to grow (same as the old server)
- Fruit trees take 10 times longer to grow
- Normal trees take 22.5 times longer to grow
- Animals take 50% longer to grow
- Cheese take 2 times longer to age
- The cellar has been buffed by 80%

### Ores
- Deep veins are 10 times rarer, but denser and 2 times bigger
- Normal and surface veins are 8 times rarer
- Copper and tetrahedrite surface and normal veins are an exception:
  their rarity hasn't changed but their density is greatly reduced
- Veins in the beneath are 12 times rarer, but denser and 3 times bigger
- Kaolin clay is no longer obtainable in the beneath
- Graphite no longer generates in the overworld

### Maps
- Xaero's map and minimap are now unlocked by crating a pocket GPS after reaching the moon
- Map atlases, displaying a minimap and coordinates, are available in the mechanical age
- The FTB chunks map has been disabled and the claim manager has been resized

### Balancing
- Rowboats are now made with bronze instead of iron
- Sloops are now made with iron instead of steel
- Death results in a temporary ban, currently set to 1 minute
- Before dying, others have 20 minutes to revive you, during which you are highlighted through walls

### Tiers
- The server has temporary locks, removing the crafting recipes for machine hulls above a certain tier
  You can view the currently locked tier and the progress towards the next one by using the `/tier` command
- Removing a lock and progressing to the next tier requires sacrificing a certain number of materials using the `/tier submit` command
  Every player contributes materials to the same pool and the lock is removed for everyone upon completion, regardles of who contributed
- Manual action from a moderator and a server restart is still required for a lock to be removed upon completion

### Other
- Coins are now available, their tooltip displays their creator and the chunk they were created in (coded by kiwi)
- The server features proximity voice and text chat, checkout the available chat commands [here](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/proximity-chat)
  Of note: use `/s <message>` to send a server-wide shout
- FTB chunks is set to allow 2 claims and 2 forceloads per player, claim protection is off, and offline chunkloading is on
